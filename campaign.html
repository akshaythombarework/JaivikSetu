Movement & Outreach

JaivikSetu Awareness Campaign

Empowering Indian grassroots farming communities with live composting demonstrations, local language pamphlets, youth college drives, and transparent market linkages.

Action Roadmap

The 4 Pillars of Grassroots Action

Our four-stage strategic rollout bridging technical research to practical rural implementation.

🚜 STAGE 1

Village Live Workshops

Organizing live community demonstrations where farmers collaboratively construct NADEP brick tanks and ferment fresh Dashaparni Ark on village common land.

Target: 250+ Village Panchayats
📑 STAGE 2

Regional Language Pamphlets

Printing and circulating illustrated, clear Marathi and Hindi recipe cards and scheme application forms across weekly rural agricultural haats and mandis.

Target: 50,000+ Printed Pocket Guides
🎓 STAGE 3

School & College Drives

Setting up balcony kitchen waste composting systems and biological soil labs in schools and agricultural colleges to cultivate the next generation of agroecologists.

Target: 100+ Youth Green Clubs
📦 STAGE 4

FPO Links & QR Recipes

Assisting Farmer Producer Organizations with QR-tagged packaging that links consumers directly to the farmer's verified organic preparation logs and PGS certificates.

Target: 30+ Organic Clusters
