Aphids (मावा / लाही)

● Highly Effective   ○ Supplementary

Soft-bodied sap-sucking pests clustering under tender shoots and leaves, excreting sticky honeydew.

Botanical Preparation: Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Dashaparni Ark (200ml in 10L water). Repeat after 5 days.
Physical Trap / Cultural: Yellow Sticky Traps (10 per acre) at crop canopy level.
⚠️ Mandatory Safety Caution: Spray in the late evening to avoid leaf scorch and protect honeybees and pollinators.

4. Myth vs. Fact Flip Cards

Click or tap any card below to flip it and reveal the scientific agricultural reality behind common organic misconceptions.

COMMON MYTH #1

"Organic farming means permanently lower yields forever."

🔄 Click to reveal the truth
SCIENTIFIC FACT

Yield may dip 10–20% during the initial 2–3 transition years while chemically burned soil recovers its microbiome. Thereafter, high organic carbon achieves yield parity with dramatically lower input costs.

COMMON MYTH #2

"'Natural' and 'Organic' food mean the exact same thing."

🔄 Click to reveal the truth
SCIENTIFIC FACT

"Natural" is an unregulated marketing word. "Organic" is a strictly audited legal certification under FSSAI, PGS-India, and NPOP ensuring zero synthetic chemicals or GMOs.

COMMON MYTH #3

"Organic farming requires expensive packaged inputs."

🔄 Click to reveal the truth
SCIENTIFIC FACT

Over 85% of organic inputs (Jeevamrut, Dashaparni, vermicompost, cow dung slurry) are prepared directly on the farm using local crop residues and native cattle, saving thousands in dealer debt.

COMMON MYTH #4

"Composting always generates foul, sickening smells."

🔄 Click to reveal the truth
SCIENTIFIC FACT

A well-managed aerobic compost pile with a 2:1 brown-to-green ratio smells sweet and earthy like monsoon rain on forest soil. Foul smells only happen when excess moisture causes oxygen starvation.

🔄 Click to flip back
COMMON MYTH #5

"Organic sprays are 100% harmless and can be sprayed anytime."

🔄 Click to reveal the truth
SCIENTIFIC FACT

Botanical extracts like Dashaparni and garlic-chilli are powerful. If sprayed in bright midday sun, they can scorch foliage and harm visiting honeybees. Always spray after 4:30 PM.

🔄 Click to flip back
COMMON MYTH #6

"Organic farming cannot produce enough food to feed India."

🔄 Click to reveal the truth
SCIENTIFIC FACT

By combining legume crop rotations, multi-cropping, water-saving mulches, and indigenous seeds, organic agroecology boosts cumulative annual farm calorie yield while preserving groundwater.

🔄 Click to flip back
COMMON MYTH #7

"Earthworms can digest all kitchen and animal waste."

🔄 Click to reveal the truth
SCIENTIFIC FACT

Earthworms (Eisenia fetida) lack lungs and breathe through skin. Citrus lemon rinds, hot chili, onions, and raw meat create fatal acidity and heat. Organic waste must be pre-decomposed first.

🔄 Click to flip back
COMMON MYTH #8

"Organic certification is only for wealthy corporate farms."

🔄 Click to reveal the truth
SCIENTIFIC FACT

The Government of India's PGS-India programme was created specifically for smallholder and marginal farmers with zero third-party audit fees based on village peer verification.
