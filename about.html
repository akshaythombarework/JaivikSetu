Our Purpose

About JaivikSetu

"Jaivik" = Organic. "Setu" = Bridge. Building an accessible, credible digital bridge between traditional agricultural wisdom and modern ecological science.

Foundational Vision

Bridging India to Chemical-Free Abundance

India is home to the world's largest concentration of organic producers, yet millions of small and marginal farmers remain trapped in cycles of chemical debt, depleted soil biology, and confusing certification bureaucracy.

JaivikSetu was created as an open-access public awareness hub. We strip away academic jargon and expensive commercial sales pitches, offering farmers and citizens transparent recipes, diagnostic calculators, and official scheme navigation.

Core Pillars of JaivikSetu:

  • ✓ 100% Non-Commercial: Zero proprietary input sales, zero affiliate commissions, and zero sponsored biases.
  • ✓ Farmer-Centric Simplicity: Short, actionable sentences with regional language translations (English & Marathi).
  • ✓ Rigorous Scientific Honesty: Transparently explaining the 2–3 transition years rather than promising unrealistic overnight miracles.
  • ✓ Government Alignment: Direct links and assistance with official portals (PKVY, NMNF, PGS-India, FSSAI).

References & Authoritative Sources

JaivikSetu content is cross-verified against peer-reviewed agricultural science and official statutory publications.

  • ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research): Package of Practices for Organic Farming in Important Crops, National Institute of Organic Farming (NIOF).
  • APEDA (Agricultural & Processed Food Products Export Development Authority): National Programme for Organic Production (NPOP) Standards and Trade Statistics (2024–2025).
  • Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India: Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY) & National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF) Operational Guidelines.
  • IFOAM – Organics International: The Four Principles of Organic Agriculture: Health, Ecology, Fairness, and Care.
  • FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India): Food Safety and Standards (Organic Foods) Regulations and Jaivik Bharat Certification Manual.
  • FiBL (Research Institute of Organic Agriculture) & IFOAM: "The World of Organic Agriculture: Statistics & Emerging Trends".
  • Pandharipande, N.D. (Kaka): Original Engineering Specifications of the NADEP Composting Tank Method, Prayog Parivar, Maharashtra.

Official Educational & Legal Disclaimer

JaivikSetu is an educational awareness portal created to support public learning and agroecological awareness. All recommendations, recipes (NSKE, Dashaparni, Jeevamrut), and calculation outputs are based on published agricultural extension guidelines and represent standard indicative formulations.

Because local microclimates, soil chemistry (pH, salinity), crop susceptibility, and pest pressures vary significantly across India's diverse agro-climatic zones, farmers are advised to conduct patch tests on a small plot and consult their regional Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), District Agriculture Officer, or State Agriculture University before making whole-farm operational decisions.
