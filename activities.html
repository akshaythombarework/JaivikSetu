Ground Engagement & Research

Our Activities & Field Experiences

Our project focused on understanding awareness and adoption of organic farming practices among farmers. We combined research, field visits, farmer interaction, surveys and awareness activities to understand the practical challenges associated with organic farming.

Each activity helped us explore a different aspect of the topic, from observing existing farming practices to preparing a digital resource that makes organic farming information easier to access.

ACTIVITY 1

1. Research & Organic Farming Awareness

Understanding Organic Farming Beyond Theory

We began by studying organic farming methods, their potential benefits and their role in sustainable agriculture. Our research covered natural manure, composting, crop residue reuse, mulching, crop rotation and natural pest management.

We also explored why farmers may find it difficult to adopt certain practices, even when they understand their benefits. This helped us connect the theoretical aspects of organic farming with the questions we wanted to investigate during our field activities.

📋 What We Did
  • ✓ Studied different organic farming methods and their applications.
  • ✓ Explored the importance of soil health, biodiversity and natural resource management.
  • ✓ Prepared awareness material and shared information about organic farming with farmers.
  • ✓ Identified the importance of practical guidance alongside general awareness.
💡 Key Learning

"Understanding a farming practice is only the first step. Knowing how to apply it under actual farming conditions is equally important."

ACTIVITY 2

2. Field Visit – Hanumanwadi, Alandi Devachi, Pune

Understanding Farming Practices at the Ground Level

Our field visit to Hanumanwadi, Alandi Devachi, gave us an opportunity to interact with local farmers and understand their day-to-day farming practices. We spoke with farmers about their crops, use of organic materials, pest management and the challenges they face while managing their farms.

The visit helped us understand that farming practices depend on several factors, including available resources, water conditions, crop requirements and cost. We observed that traditional methods and modern agricultural inputs can be used together on the same farm.

🌾 What We Observed
  • ✓ Soybean and ghevda were among the crops grown in the area, while jowar was cultivated during summer.
  • ✓ Some farmers used taag as green manure and animal waste for preparing farm manure.
  • ✓ Crop residues were reused, while chemical pesticides were also used for pest control.
  • ✓ Some farmers used natural materials such as neem and cow urine.
  • ✓ Rainfall and available water resources influenced farming conditions.
🌱 Experience & Learning

The discussions helped us understand that farmers make practical decisions based on their circumstances. They may be aware of natural farming methods but still have questions about their preparation, use and suitability.

We realised that encouraging organic farming requires more than explaining its benefits. Farmers also need understandable information about how different methods work and how they can be used appropriately.

ACTIVITY 3

3. Farmer Interview – Vidhate Natural Farm, Manjari Budruk

Learning from Natural Farming and Biodiversity

Our visit to Vidhate Natural Farm provided an opportunity to learn from a practitioner involved in natural farming. The interview helped us understand the importance of traditional plant varieties, natural resource reuse and biodiversity in agricultural practices.

The farm included different varieties of vegetables, fruits, flowers and medicinal plants, including some traditional and rare plants that were unfamiliar to us. We also learned about methods used to manage organic materials and maintain a diverse farm environment.

🍃 What We Learned
  • ✓ The importance of conserving indigenous and rare plant varieties.
  • ✓ How fallen leaves and pruned branches can be decomposed and reused as organic material.
  • ✓ The role of beneficial insects and flowering plants in the farm ecosystem.
  • ✓ The importance of preserving traditional agricultural knowledge.
  • ✓ The challenges of conserving farmland and rare plants amid urban expansion.
🌱 Experience & Learning

One of the most interesting aspects of the visit was understanding how materials that might otherwise be discarded can be put to use in farming. We also learned that natural farming involves more than selecting a particular fertiliser or pest-control method; it requires attention to the wider farm ecosystem.

The visit gave us a practical perspective on biodiversity conservation and showed how individual farming practices can contribute to the responsible use of natural resources.

ACTIVITY 4

4. Farmer Awareness & Survey – Lalgun Village

Understanding Awareness, Adoption and Farmers' Needs

To explore our main subtopic more closely, we prepared a questionnaire for farmers in Lalgun village. The survey focused on awareness of organic farming, current farming practices, practical knowledge, willingness to adopt more organic methods and the difficulties farmers associate with adoption.

The questions were kept simple, using Yes/No responses, one-word answers and short responses. This made it easier to organise the information and compare responses across the questionnaire.

Alongside the survey activity, we also shared information about organic farming with farmers as part of our awareness efforts.

📊 What We Did
  • ✓ Prepared a questionnaire focused on awareness and adoption of organic farming.
  • ✓ Included questions about organic manure, crop residue reuse and natural pest management.
  • ✓ Examined the use of chemical pesticides and previous experience with organic methods.
  • ✓ Asked about practical knowledge, information gaps and adoption barriers.
  • ✓ Organised the recorded responses and prepared charts to compare different aspects of the survey.
🔍 What the Survey Explored

The survey considered four main areas: awareness of organic farming, current adoption of practices, practical knowledge and difficulties associated with adoption. These areas helped us organise the collected responses and examine the relationship between knowing about organic farming and using its practices.

🌱 Experience & Learning

The activity helped us understand the importance of collecting information directly from the community and organising it systematically. It also showed why awareness, actual use, practical knowledge and willingness should be considered separately when studying organic farming adoption.

ACTIVITY 5

5. Digital Initiative – JaivikSetu

Connecting Organic Farming Information with Practical Guidance

JaivikSetu is our digital initiative developed to make organic farming information easier to access and understand. The website brings together explanations of farming methods, preparation guides, natural pest-control information, sustainable field practices and details about government schemes and certification.

The idea came from our overall project focus: awareness becomes more useful when people can also find practical information about the methods they want to understand.

⚡ Key Features
  • ✓ Organic Farming Learning: Introduces the principles of organic agriculture, soil health and biodiversity.
  • ✓ Composting Guides: Covers heap or pit composting, NADEP, vermicomposting, kitchen waste composting, Bokashi, green manuring and biochar.
  • ✓ Natural Pest Management: Provides information about neem-based preparations, Dashaparni Ark, garlic-chilli preparations, biological pest-control options and traps.
  • ✓ Field Practices: Explains mulching, crop rotation, mixed cropping, intercropping and drip irrigation.
  • ✓ Interactive Tools: Includes a compost calculator, natural pest finder, practice-selection quiz and Myth vs Fact cards.
  • ✓ Schemes & Certification: Introduces relevant government schemes and organic certification systems.
  • ✓ Regional-Language Access: Provides content in English and Marathi, along with printable guides.
🌱 Experience & Learning

Developing JaivikSetu helped us bring information from different parts of organic farming into one organised platform. We focused on presenting preparation processes and practical guidance alongside basic explanations so users could explore a topic in greater detail.

The website is intended as an educational resource. Users should still refer to suitable agricultural guidance for crop-specific recommendations, particularly when preparing and applying pest-control solutions.

ACTIVITY 6

6. Article Writing – Sharing Our Findings

Communicating the Importance of Organic Farming Awareness

As part of our project, we prepared a Marathi article titled “सेंद्रिय शेतीचा स्वीकार वाढवण्यासाठी जागरूकतेसोबत व्यवहार्य मार्गदर्शनही गरजेचे” (Practical Guidance Alongside Awareness Is Essential to Increase the Adoption of Organic Farming).

The article brought together our research, field observations, farmer interaction and understanding of the challenges associated with adopting organic farming practices.

✍️ What We Did
  • ✓ Organised information gathered during the different project activities.
  • ✓ Included observations from the field visit and interview.
  • ✓ Discussed the relationship between awareness, practical knowledge and adoption.
  • ✓ Prepared the article in Marathi for submission to a newspaper.
🌱 Experience & Learning

Article writing helped us present the project in a form that could be understood by a wider audience. It also encouraged us to connect our field experiences with the broader need for practical agricultural awareness.

The article reflects the central idea of our project: sharing information is important, but explaining how organic practices can be applied is equally necessary.

Reflections & Insights

Overall Project Learning

Our activities gave us an opportunity to study organic farming from different perspectives: research, direct interaction, practical observation, survey-based analysis and information sharing.

The field visit helped us understand the farming practices used in local conditions. The interview at Vidhate Natural Farm highlighted natural resource reuse and biodiversity, while the survey helped us organise questions about awareness, current practices, knowledge and adoption barriers. Our awareness material, article and JaivikSetu website provided ways to communicate information beyond the research itself.

Together, these activities reinforced the importance of connecting awareness with practical knowledge. Organic farming practices need to be explained clearly, with appropriate preparation guidance and consideration of the conditions in which farmers work.

Through this project, we aimed to understand farmers' experiences, share useful information and make organic farming knowledge more accessible.

🌱
Our central takeaway:
"Increasing awareness is an important beginning; making organic farming practices easier to understand and adopt is the next step."
