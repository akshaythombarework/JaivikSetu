Government Schemes & Organic Certification

Demystifying national subsidies, zero-cost peer certification for small farmers, export standards, and authentic organic labelling standards in India.

The 4 Central Pillars

Key National Programmes & Certifications

Government of India initiatives supporting organic transitions from soil preparation to retail branding.

MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE

1. PKVY (Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana)

Flagship cluster approach program promoting chemical-free organic farming across 20-hectare village blocks.

Financial Incentive: ₹50,000 per hectare over 3 years. Of this, ₹31,000 (62%) is transferred via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into farmer bank accounts for on-farm organic inputs, bio-fertilizers, and botanical extracts.
Value Addition & Marketing: ₹8,800/ha provided for post-harvest handling, cluster grading, organic packaging, and local organic haat transportation.
Official Portal: pgsindia-ncof.gov.in ↗
CHEMICAL-FREE CROPPING

2. NMNF (National Mission on Natural Farming)

Standalone mission focusing on on-farm cow-based natural inputs (Jeevamrut, Beejamrut, Ghanjeevamrut) along the Ganga corridor and pan-India.

Bio-Input Resource Centers (BRCs): Establishment of 15,000 community resource centers across gram panchayats providing ready-made natural sprays and master trainer farmer demonstrations.
Kisan Call Centers & KVKs: Direct farmer training via Krishi Vigyan Kendras with zero synthetic fertilizers allowed within 5km river corridors.
Official Portal: naturalfarming.dac.gov.in ↗
PEER CERTIFICATION

3. PGS-India (Participatory Guarantee System)

Locally focused, grassroots quality assurance system. Certifies producers based on active peer-review by neighbouring farmers with zero exorbitant auditing fees.

Smallholder Friendly: Solves the barrier of expensive private third-party certification. Minimum 5 farmers form a local group with shared mutual inspection visits.
Green vs Organic Logo: 'PGS-India Green' granted during transition years 1–3; 'PGS-India Organic' granted on full 3-year chemical-free completion.
Official Portal: pgsindia-ncof.gov.in ↗
EXPORT EQUIVALENCE & RETAIL

4. NPOP & Jaivik Bharat Certification

Statutory standards governing commercial packaged organic goods across retail shelves and international export borders.

NPOP (APEDA): Required for international export to USA, EU, and Switzerland. Audited by accredited third-party certification bodies (e.g. Aditi Organic, OneCert).
Jaivik Bharat Mark: Mandatory unified logo issued by FSSAI for all organic food sold in India. Look for the green circle sprout on food packaging.
Official Portal: apeda.gov.in ↗
FSSAI Unified Trust Symbol
Consumer Trust Mark

Understanding the "Jaivik Bharat" Logo

The Government of India and the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) introduced the Jaivik Bharat logo to eliminate market confusion between unregulated "natural/chemical-free" claims and legally certified organic food.

🌿 The Symbolism: The green circle represents the ecological biosphere; the sprouting leaf represents emerging organic regeneration; and the golden center represents healthy farm harvest.
⚖️ Legal Mandate: Under Food Safety and Standards (Organic Foods) Regulations 2017, no food product can be sold as "Organic" in Indian retail stores without displaying this authenticated Jaivik Bharat mark alongside the FSSAI license.

Step-by-Step Pathway

5-Step PGS-India Certification Roadmap

Follow this clear roadmap to enroll your farm cluster and achieve official certified organic status.

1

1. Form a Peer Group

Gather at least 5 local farmers in your village committed to chemical-free farming.

2

2. Register on PGS Portal

Register your Local Group (LG) on the official pgsindia-ncof.gov.in portal or through local KVK.

3

3. Peer Inspection & Diary

Maintain farm activity diaries and conduct mutual peer farm inspection rounds.

4

4. Conversion Years (Green)

Receive 'PGS-Green' certificate during the 2–3 year soil detoxification and stabilization period.

5

5. Certified Organic Status

After 36 continuous months of verified organic inputs, receive full 'PGS-Organic' certificate.
