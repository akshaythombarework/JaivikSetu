Active Pest Match

Aphids (मावा / लाही)

● High Effectiveness   ○ Supplementary

Soft-bodied sap-sucking pests clustering under tender shoots and leaves, excreting sticky honeydew that triggers sooty mold.

Recommended Botanical Recipe: Spray 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Dashaparni Ark (200ml in 10L water). Repeat after 5 days.
Trap & Cultural Practice: Yellow Sticky Traps (10 per acre) installed at crop canopy level.
⚠️ Mandatory Safety Rule: Spray in the late evening (after 4:30 PM) to avoid leaf scorch and protect honeybees and pollinators.
Authentic Formulations

6 Botanical & Biological Recipe Cards

Time-tested natural crop formulations with ingredient lists, preparation steps, and application dosages.

🧴
BROAD SPECTRUM REPELLENT & IGR

1. Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%)

The premier botanical antifeedant, insect growth regulator, and oviposition deterrent containing pure Azadirachtin.

Ingredients: 5 kg dry neem seed kernels, 100L clean water, 200g natural khadi soap (emulsifier).
Preparation: Pound neem kernels into fine powder. Soak in 10L water overnight in muslin cloth bag. Squeeze extract into 100L water with dissolved soap.
Target Pests: Helicoverpa caterpillars, aphids, whiteflies, leaf miners, and thrips.
Dose: Spray 500ml NSKE per 10L knapsack sprayer in late evening.
🌿
10 MEDICINAL LEAVES FERMENTED POTENT

2. Dashaparni Ark (10-Leaf Decoction)

Potent Ayurvedic fermented pest repellent combining 10 bitter and medicinal leaves with indigenous cow urine.

Ingredients: Leaves of Neem, Karanj, Custard apple, Castor, Datura, Papaya, Marigold, Guava, Nirgundi, and Lantana (2 kg each) + 5 kg cow dung + 10L indigenous cow urine + 200L water.
Preparation: Crush leaves, place in plastic drum with cow dung and urine. Ferment for 30–40 days in shade, stirring clockwise twice daily. Filter through fine muslin cloth.
Target Pests: Stem borers, leaf folders, sucking pests, and early instar caterpillars.
Dose: 200–250ml per 10L water. Shelf life up to 6 months in shade.
🌶️
CONTACT IRRITANT MITES & THRIPS

3. Garlic-Chilli-Ginger Extract

Intense pungent contact spray using allicin and capsaicin to repel stubborn sucking insects and mites.

Ingredients: 500g garlic cloves, 500g spicy green chillies, 250g fresh ginger, 50g mild soap, 10L water.
Preparation: Grind garlic and chillies separately into fine paste. Soak overnight in water. Boil gently for 15 minutes, cool, strain through muslin cloth, and add soap sticker.
Target Pests: Red spider mites, yellow thrips, whiteflies, and aphids on cotton & vegetables.
Dose: Dilute 500ml stock extract in 15L water. Wet underside of leaves.
🥛
MICROBIAL INOCULANT COW-BASED

4. Jeevamrut, Beejamrut & Panchagavya

Fermented indigenous cow-derived microbial tonics that multiply billions of aerobic microbes per milliliter.

Jeevamrut: 10 kg desi cow dung + 10L cow urine + 2 kg jaggery + 2 kg pulse flour (besan) + handful of virgin forest soil in 200L water. Ferment 5–7 days.
Beejamrut: Seed treatment concoction protecting young germinating seeds against soil-borne wilt and damping off.
Panchagavya: 5 sacred bovine products (milk, curd, ghee, dung, urine) + banana + jaggery for plant growth vigor.
Dose: 200L Jeevamrut per acre via drip or flood irrigation twice a month.
🔬
LIVING ANTAGONISTS BIO-PESTICIDES

5. Biological Parasitoids & Fungi

Harnessing friendly living organisms to parasitise insect eggs and destroy destructive fungal root pathogens.

Trichogramma Chilonis: Tiny egg parasitoid wasps released via egg cards (50,000/ha). Parasitises caterpillar eggs before they can hatch.
Trichoderma Viride / Harzianum: Antagonistic soil fungus that devours Fusarium, Rhizoctonia, and Pythium root rot.
Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt): Biological protein crystals toxic strictly to caterpillars when ingested.
Dose: Mix 1kg Trichoderma in 100kg farm compost for soil application.
🪤
NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL TRAPPING

6. Traps, Lures & Trap Crops

Eco-friendly mechanical defenses that monitor and capture adult breeding pests without spraying any liquid.

Yellow & Blue Sticky Traps: Yellow cards (10–12/acre) attract whiteflies & aphids. Blue cards attract thrips.
Pheromone Funnel Traps: Species-specific female sex pheromone lures (Helilure, Spodolure) capturing male moths (5/acre).
Trap Crops (Marigold & Maize): African marigolds planted around tomato crops lure away fruit borers and suppress root nematodes.
Installation: Erect traps 1 ft above crop canopy; replace lures every 25 days.
